Overview

What is off-grid solar?

An off-grid system is disconnected from the public grid. PV generates DC, an MPPT controller charges a LiFePO4 bank, and a pure-sine inverter supplies 230 V AC. Built for farmhouses, mountain resorts, rural clinics, towers, and sites with 6–12 hour daily outages.

  • High-wattage PV strings for low-light yield
  • MPPT charge control — up to 30% more into the bank than PWM
  • LiFePO4 with 15-year life, 90% DoD, BMS thermal protection
  • Not eligible for PM Surya Ghar CFA (on-grid / hybrid are)
