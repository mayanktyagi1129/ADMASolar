Long-Term Maintenance & Support

Your Solar Investment. Protected for the Long Run.

Installing a solar system is only the beginning. To continue getting reliable performance from your system, periodic inspection, preventive maintenance and timely attention to potential issues are important.

With ADMA Solar Annual Maintenance & Support, you get the confidence of knowing that your solar system is being cared for beyond installation.

Keep Your Solar System Performing at Its Best

Our AMC is designed to help identify potential issues early and keep your solar system operating safely, efficiently and reliably.

Depending on the selected service plan and system configuration, AMC support may include:

  • Periodic solar system health checks
  • Inspection of solar panels and mounting structure
  • Inspection of inverter and other key equipment
  • Electrical connection and cable checks
  • System performance review
  • Generation and monitoring assessment
  • Identification of visible faults or abnormalities
  • Preventive maintenance recommendations
  • Scheduled service visits
  • Remote or on-site technical support, as applicable
