Knowledge base & help centre

Frequently asked questions.

Going solar is a long-term investment. Here are the 57 questions we hear most often — savings, system choice, subsidy, backup, and maintenance.

Getting started

1. Is solar right for my home or business?

In most cases, yes — if you have suitable space and sunlight. We start from your consumption, roof, shading, connection type, and future loads before recommending a system.

2. What size solar system do I need?

Capacity follows consumption pattern, rooftop, sanctioned load, future needs, and topology — not only the monthly bill. Share a bill and we size it.

3. How much electricity can a solar system generate?

Generation depends on kW, location, weather, orientation, shading, and design. We give a site-specific estimate rather than a generic CUF.

4. How much rooftop space do I need?

About 80–100 sq. ft. of usable, shadow-free area per kW. Panel type, layout, and structure change the number.

5. What if my rooftop has shade?

Partial shade does not always rule solar out. A survey maps the shade pattern and the layout that still works.

6. Can solar be installed on an existing building?

Yes, on most residential, commercial, and industrial roofs, subject to structure, space, shade, and access.

7. Can panels go on a metal shed or elevated structure?

Yes, on suitable sheds or designed elevated MMS, subject to wind, strength, and how the space below is used.

8. What if my consumption increases later?

Tell us about ACs, EVs, or other loads at design time so we leave a path to expand.

Choosing the system

9. What is an on-grid solar system?

It runs with the DISCOM and is built to cut bills. Surplus treatment follows the utility’s net-metering rules. See On-grid.

10. What is a hybrid solar system?

Solar + grid + battery. Bill savings and backup during outages. See Hybrid.

11. What is an off-grid solar system?

Independent of the grid, with batteries. For sites without a reliable DISCOM line. See Off-grid.

12. Which type is right for me?

On-grid for bill reduction. Hybrid if backup matters. Off-grid where the grid is absent or unreliable.

13. Will on-grid work during a power cut?

No. Anti-islanding shuts it down so line workers stay safe. Hybrid with battery keeps selected loads on.

14. How does battery backup work?

Hybrid stores surplus in LiFePO4. Runtime depends on bank size and the loads you protect.

15. Can I add a battery later?

Often yes, if the inverter and original design allow it. Better to plan the path before install.

16. Can I expand later?

Depends on roof, inverter headroom, electrics, and regulations. Raise it at design stage.

Cost & savings

17. How much does a rooftop system cost?

Capacity, topology, equipment, roof, and batteries all move the price. The right system is neither always the cheapest nor the largest. See the calculator.

18. How much can I save?

Savings follow consumption, tariff, generation, size, and DISCOM settlement. We estimate from your actual bills.

19. How long to recover the investment?

It varies with cost, tariff, usage, yield, and CFA. Treat solar as a 25-year asset, not only year-one payback.

20. Will I still get an electricity bill?

Usually yes — fixed charges and residual units remain. Solar can cut the bill sharply; it does not guarantee a zero bill every month.

21. What if I generate more than I use?

Eligible grid-tied systems export or settle surplus under DISCOM rules, which vary by state.

22. Are EMI / financing options available?

Bank, NBFC, and partner products may be available, including zero-down EMI from about ₹1,499/month. Ask the team for current offers.

23. Is a bigger system always better?

No. Oversizing raises capex without matching financial benefit. Size to present and planned loads.

24. How do I know if solar is a good decision?

Stack bills, consumption, roof, expected yield, and project cost together. We walk the numbers before you commit.

Subsidy, approvals & net metering

25. Can I get a government subsidy?

Eligible residential consumers may receive CFA under PM Surya Ghar, subject to current MNRE / DISCOM rules.

26. How much subsidy can I receive?

Current residential CFA: ₹30,000 (1 kW), ₹60,000 (2 kW), ₹78,000 (3 kW and above). Limits can change.

27. Does ADMA help with the subsidy process?

Yes — documentation, portal filing, and tracking until credit. Final release stays with the authorities.

28. What is net metering?

A utility mechanism that accounts for rooftop generation against grid import, per DISCOM rules.

29. How does net metering work?

A bi-directional meter records import and export. Settlement follows your DISCOM’s tariff order.

30. What approvals are required?

Depends on location, connection, and system type — technical sanction, meter change, and scheme papers.

31. How long do approvals take?

Utility workload and paperwork drive the timeline. We guide; some clocks sit with DISCOM.

32. What documents will I need?

We issue a project-specific checklist from your connection and scheme path.

Installation

33. What is the install process?

Needs → bill analysis → site assessment → design → proposal → documentation → install → commission → handover.

34. How long does installation take?

Physical residential install is often a few days; 48-hour install after layout approval on standard roofs. Full timeline includes approvals and metering.

35. Will install damage my roof?

A designed MMS is meant to secure panels without harming the roof. Survey picks the method.

36. Will power be interrupted?

Most work proceeds without outage. A short cut may be needed at electrical connection.

37. Do I need to be present?

Not the whole time. Access and a coordinating person should be available.

38. Who installs the system?

ADMA crews install to the approved design — a complete solution, not loose equipment.

Performance

39. Will panels work on cloudy days?

Yes, at lower output than a clear day.

40. Do they work in rain?

Yes, when there is daylight. Rain can rinse dust; it is not a substitute for cleaning.

41. Do they generate at night?

No. Night loads come from the grid or from batteries on hybrid / off-grid.

42. Does heat affect panels?

They are built for outdoors; very high cell temperature trims watts. Design and module choice matter in Indian summers — we spec TOPCon for that.

43. How does dust affect generation?

Dust cuts light to the cells. Clean to the local dust and bird load.

44. What if a panel is partly shaded?

Shade can drag that panel and, depending on stringing, neighbours. Layout minimises it.

45. Can I monitor the system?

Yes — inverter apps track generation and status on your phone.

Maintenance & warranty

46. How much maintenance is required?

Low. Periodic cleaning and occasional electrical / structural checks.

47. How often should panels be cleaned?

Depends on dust, pollution, birds, and rain. Inspect and clean when soiled — AMC can schedule it.

48. How long do panels last?

Decades, with gradual output fade covered by manufacturer performance warranties (typically 25 years).

49. What warranties are provided?

Panels, inverter, batteries, and MMS each have their own terms, listed in your project pack.

50. What if an inverter or part fails?

We diagnose and run the warranty / service path for that equipment.

51. Do you support after installation?

Yes — guidance and assistance through the life of the plant, per the service scope.
