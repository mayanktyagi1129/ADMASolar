⚡ Utility Synchronized

On-Grid (Grid-Tied) Solar Architecture

An On-Grid solar system runs in sync with your local electric discom utility grid. During daytime hours, solar PV modules power your home appliances directly. Excess solar energy produced is automatically exported to the grid through a bi-directional Net Meter, generating power credits on your utility electricity bill.

Core Operating Mechanism:

  1. Solar Photon Generation: High-efficiency mono PERC panels capture sunlight and output Direct Current (DC) electricity.
  2. Inverter Transformation: Pure Sine Wave Grid-Tied Inverter converts DC into 230V Single-Phase or 415V Three-Phase AC power synchronized to grid voltage and frequency.
  3. Dual-Flow Bi-Directional Net Metering: If home demand is lower than solar generation, surplus units flow into the DISCOM grid. At night, your home draws power back from the grid, offsetting daytime credits.

🔋 100% Autonomous Storage

Off-Grid Independent Solar Architecture

Off-Grid solar systems are completely self-sustaining energy solutions operating independent of the public power grid. They utilize heavy-duty battery storage banks (Lithium LFP or Tubular Gel) to store energy collected during daylight for 24-hour uninterrupted operation.

Core Operating Mechanism:

  1. MPPT Charge Regulation: Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT) solar charge controllers regulate voltage from solar panels to safely charge deep-cycle battery banks.
  2. Chemical Energy Storage: High-capacity LiFePO4 batteries store clean electrical energy with 95%+ round-trip efficiency and 6,000+ cycle lifespan.
  3. Autonomous Inversion: Off-grid inverter delivers clean 230V AC output to run heavy household loads (refrigerators, pumps, ACs, lights) with zero grid dependence.

🔄 Bi-Directional Hybrid Power

Hybrid (Grid-Tie + Battery) Architecture

Hybrid solar systems offer the ultimate peace of mind by unifying the grid-tied financial bill savings of Net Metering with the emergency backup capabilities of lithium batteries. If grid power fails, intelligent Automatic Transfer Switches shift home loads to battery power in less than 10 milliseconds.

Core Operating Mechanism:

  1. Smart Load Prioritization: Daytime solar energy powers home loads first, charges the battery second, and exports any remaining excess to the DISCOM grid.
  2. Instant Outage Switching: Built-in UPS transition logic keeps critical appliances, computers, and medical equipment running continuously during grid blackouts.
  3. Time-Of-Use Tariff Shaving: Intelligent inverter firmware discharges stored battery energy during high-cost peak utility tariff hours.

Solar FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to essential solar installation queries.

For safety reasons, On-Grid solar inverters feature an automated Anti-Islanding Protection mechanism. When the utility grid power fails, the inverter automatically shuts down within milliseconds to prevent backfeeding high-voltage electricity into grid lines where DISCOM utility technicians may be carrying out repair work. If power cuts are frequent in your area, consider a Hybrid Solar System with battery storage.

Net Metering is a DISCOM billing agreement that uses a bi-directional electricity meter to measure both power imported from the grid and excess solar electricity exported to the grid. At the end of every monthly billing cycle, your net electricity bill is calculated as [Units Consumed - Units Exported]. If exports exceed consumption, credits roll over to subsequent months or are reimbursed at DISCOM feed-in rates.

Tier-1 Mono PERC / TOPCon solar panels come with a 25-Year Performance Warranty (retaining 80%+ generation output after 25 years). Grid inverters typically last 10-12 years. Modern Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) solar batteries last 10 to 15 years (6,000+ deep discharge cycles), whereas traditional lead-acid solar tubular batteries last 4 to 6 years.
