Overview

What is an On-Grid Solar System?

An On-Grid solar system (also referred to as Grid-Tied or Utility-Interactive) operates in direct synchronization with your local electricity provider (DISCOM). Unlike off-grid setups, On-Grid systems do not require expensive battery banks to store power.

Instead, your home acts as a micro-power generator during daylight hours. When your solar panels produce more energy than your home consumes, the surplus electricity is exported to the public power grid through a DISCOM-approved Bi-Directional Net Meter.

💡 Key Advantage of Net Metering:

You receive monthly electricity credits for every kWh exported to the grid. At night or during cloudy hours, you draw power back from the grid, and the meter nets off your consumption against exported units.

How On-Grid Solar Works (Step-by-Step)

1

Solar PV Generation

Monocrystalline panels capture sunlight and generate DC electricity on your rooftop.

2

Inverter Conversion

Grid-tied inverter transforms DC into 230V AC current matching grid phase and frequency.

3

Home Appliance Powering

AC electricity powers lights, ACs, refrigerators, and appliances inside your property.

4

Bi-Directional Net Export

Unused surplus electricity is pushed into the grid, earning you monetary credits on your power bill.

✓ Advantages of On-Grid Solar

  • ✓ Lowest System Price: No expensive battery replacements, making capital entry 40% cheaper than off-grid.
  • ✓ Govt PM Surya Ghar Subsidy: Direct bank transfer subsidy up to ₹78,000 for residential homes.
  • ✓ Highest ROI: Recovers initial investment in just 3 to 4 years, followed by 20+ years of free electricity.
  • ✓ Zero Maintenance: Requires only periodic panel surface cleaning with water.

⚠️ System Considerations & Limits

  • ✕ Shuts Off During Outages: Cannot run during grid power failure due to safety Anti-Islanding grid lockout rules.
  • ✕ Requires Grid Connection: Cannot operate in remote areas without a active DISCOM grid power line.
  • ✕ DISCOM Approval Required: Net metering sanction approval needed from state power utility department.
