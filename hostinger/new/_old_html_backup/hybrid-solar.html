Overview

What is a Hybrid Solar System?

A Hybrid solar system unifies solar panels, a bi-directional hybrid inverter, modular lithium battery wall storage, and the utility discom grid into a single intelligent power microgrid.

Standard On-Grid systems are forced to shut down during blackouts to protect grid workers (anti-islanding). A Hybrid system solves this completely: when grid power goes down, the intelligent hybrid inverter immediately isolates the home microgrid and continues powering your loads from solar and batteries.

⚡ Intelligent Power Prioritization Matrix:

  • Priority 1: Solar energy powers active home appliances directly.
  • Priority 2: Surplus solar charges the local Lithium-Iron Phosphate battery bank.
  • Priority 3: Remaining solar generation is exported to the utility grid via Net Metering.

How Hybrid Solar Works (Dual Mode)

1

Daytime Grid-Connected Mode

Solar powers loads, tops up the battery storage, and exports excess units to DISCOM for monthly bill credits.

2

Instant Blackout Isolation (<10ms)

Automatic Transfer Switch isolates grid connection and transfers home loads to battery in less than 10 milliseconds.

3

Nighttime Battery Discharge

Stored daytime green energy runs lights, fans, TVs, and refrigerators, reducing expensive night grid utility rates.

4

Peak Tariff Load Shaving

Smart IoT firmware automatically avoids drawing power from the grid during high peak-tariff hours.

Core Components of a Hybrid Solar System

Next-generation smart power control equipment.

Bi-directional inverter with dual MPPTs, built-in grid tie export control, battery charger, and emergency UPS transfer relay.

Modular Lithium Battery Wall

Sleek, wall-mountable LiFePO4 battery modules (5kWh to 30kWh) with smart active cell balancing and 15-year operational life.

Bi-Directional Net Meter

Measures surplus solar energy sent to the local power utility grid for end-of-month electricity bill offset credits.

IoT Mobile Monitoring App

Real-time smartphone telemetry tracking solar yield, battery charge level, home power consumption, and monthly cost savings.
