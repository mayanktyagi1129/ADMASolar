How it works

Priority: loads, then battery, then export.

A hybrid inverter unifies PV, LiFePO4 wall storage, and the DISCOM into one microgrid. When the grid drops, the inverter isolates the house and keeps selected loads on solar and battery — on-grid systems cannot do this.

  • Priority 1 — solar powers active appliances
  • Priority 2 — surplus charges the LiFePO4 bank
  • Priority 3 — remainder exported via net meter
  • Night discharge and peak-tariff load shaving via IoT firmware
