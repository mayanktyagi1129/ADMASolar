Overview

What is an on-grid system?

An on-grid (grid-tied) system runs in sync with your DISCOM. There is no battery bank. Daytime surplus is exported through a DISCOM-approved bi-directional net meter; at night you draw from the grid and the meter nets the units.

  • Solar PV generation on the roof (540–590 Wp TOPCon / mono PERC)
  • Grid-tied string inverter (~98.6%) with dual MPPT, anti-islanding, Wi-Fi
  • Home loads first; surplus exported for bill credits
  • Shuts off during outages (anti-islanding safety) — choose hybrid if you need backup
